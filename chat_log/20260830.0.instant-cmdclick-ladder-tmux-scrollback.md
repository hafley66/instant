## Solution/Approach
- `src-tauri/src/refresolve.rs` (new, ~900 lines with tests) owns the whole ladder: `split_line_ref`, `looks_like_path`, `ancestors_of`, `crawl_candidates`, `repo_root_for`, `rank_exact` (returns `(path, is_whole_tail)`), `rank_fuzzy` (nucleo-matcher 0.3.1, `Config::set_match_paths`, floor of 12 points per query char), `sibling_candidates` (only dirs holding `.git`, at or above the repo root), `git_absent` (`rev-list --all -- <path>`, walks back up to 20 revisions past a delete commit), `read_git_blob`. Result is a serde-tagged union: `hit | choices | absent | miss`.
- `src/refResolve.ts` shrank to a typed call plus a 1 s dedupe. `src/0_pathLadder.ts`, its tests, `src/refResolve.test.ts` and the `fzf` npm dep were deleted.

The Tauri adapter is four lines. Migrated `refResolve.ts` and `clickrules.ts` only — 111 `invoke` call sites remain across 29 files.

## Key Insights
- A pty carries bytes; scrollback belongs to whoever keeps the lines that fell off. On the alternate screen nobody does, which is what the yellow `0/0` means. `INSTANT_DIRECT_PTY=1` (`pty.rs:47`) skips tmux and gives xterm native scrollback, trading away detach/reattach.
- `mouse_any_flag=1` means the app claimed the mouse, so tmux forwards every wheel event with `send-keys -M` and no key-table bind runs. Codex reported 1; Claude Code panes are `alt=1` and own their own scroll, which is correct.
- nucleo-matcher's `Pattern::match_list` returns `u32` and requires `T: AsRef<str>`, so scoring per entry with `pattern.score(Utf32Str::new(s, &mut buf), &mut matcher)` is the way to keep the index position.
- The newest commit touching a path is often the one that deleted it — `git_absent` must walk back until `cat-file -e <rev>:<path>` succeeds.
- Copying a Rust binary over `~/.cargo/bin` on arm64 invalidates its signature and it then runs silently with exit 0; `codesign -f -s -` repairs it.
- A symlinked `node_modules` realpaths outside the project root, so vite 403s package assets — that silently shrank the hover card and broke its screenshot until `server.fs.allow` covered the realpath.
- Repo law enforced by hooks: max 2 consecutive comment lines in new code; no negative parallelism, one-word sentences, or the stem "honest" in prose.
